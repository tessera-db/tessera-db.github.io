Why Tessera exists

One app for everything you keep track of.

Quick notes lived in one app, the to-do list in another, due dates on a calendar, and every budget, stock list and collection wanted an app of its own. Each did one job, and none of them talked to each other.

Tessera puts it all in one place. Each part of your life gets its own library of tables, set up the way you want, and the same app tracks a reading list, a small shop or a classroom.

Seven views

List, table, board, gallery, calendar, timeline or cards. Each table keeps its own view.

Real relations

Link records between tables, or relate them where two fields match. See it all in a relation diagram.

Dashboards across everything

Metrics, bars, columns, donuts, trend lines, stacked charts, heatmap pivots, ranked tables and goal rings. Group and filter by fields in any related table, with colour rules that light up when a value crosses a line.

Reminders

Put a bell on any date field. Tessera reminds you at the right time, even after a restart.

Home-screen widgets

Pin a metric, a dashboard insight or a one-tap "new record" button to your home screen.

Library locks

Hide a library behind your fingerprint, face or screen lock, so a borrowed phone stays a borrowed phone.

How it works

From one table to a whole system.

Every library starts as a single table. Add more tables, join them, put dashboards on top, and write a rule once to use it anywhere. Here's the Corner Shop sample, built up step by step.

  1. 1Tables

    Add as many tables as you need

    Tap + beside the tabs to add a table, import one from a CSV spreadsheet, or add a dashboard or a Scripts tab. Each table keeps its own fields, view, sort, filters and metric pills, so Products and Suppliers can look completely different.

    ProductsSuppliersOrdersOverviewScripts

  2. 2Relations

    Join them together

    Every order picks its product, and every product picks its supplier. Or pair records automatically wherever two fields match, including dates in the same week, month or quarter. The diagram shows how it all connects.

  3. 3Dashboards

    Add a dashboard on top

    Add a Dashboard tab and pick from fourteen visuals. Rows, values and conditions can come from any table in the library, joined through your relations, with a live preview as you build. Pin any card to your home screen.

    Write a rule once, reuse it everywhere

Where your data lives

Two places. Both of them yours.

There is no Tessera account and no Tessera server. Your libraries stay on your phone, and a copy goes to your own Google Drive only for the libraries you choose to sync.

Always

Your phone

Libraries, records, attachments and settings are kept in the app's private storage. Tessera works fully offline.

HTTPS
Optional · off by default

Your Google Drive

Turn on sync for a library and it's copied to a Tessera folder in your own Drive, kept up to date across your devices.

0
Tessera serversThe developer runs none, so your data never passes through anyone else.

No account with us

Nothing to sign up for. Google sign-in is only for Drive sync, and only if you turn it on.

No ads or trackers

No advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services.

Drive, fenced off

Tessera can only open the files it creates in your Drive. It can't see anything else there.

Pro via Google Play

An optional one-time purchase. Google Play handles payment, so Tessera never sees your card.
